How this tool works

  1. Add a local video.
  2. The app transcribes it in your browser and fills the captions editor.
  3. Edit the words or line breaks, then style the captions in Settings.
  4. Use Preview to check timing and layout.
  5. Export a captioned video, SRT, or EDL + PNG package.

No cloud upload: this tool transcribes, previews, renders, and exports directly in your browser.
